About The Club

Highland Xtreme Motorsports Club is a passionate motorsports community bringing together rally drivers, automotive enthusiasts and adventure seekers. The club organizes rally championships, motorsport training programs, automotive events and motorsport volunteering opportunities across India.

Upcoming Events

INRC - Rally of The Highlands Round - 2 (2026)

Round 2 of the Indian National Rally Championship (INRC) 2026 will be held on 22 May 2026, bringing together some of the finest rally drivers from across the country. The event promises intense competition, thrilling stages, and an electrifying atmosphere for motorsport enthusiasts. Organized under the regulations of FMSCI, the rally will showcase speed, precision, and the true spirit of Indian rallying.
